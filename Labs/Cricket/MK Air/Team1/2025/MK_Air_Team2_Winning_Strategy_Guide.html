🎮 Match Day Strategy & Execution

Pre-Match (2 hours before)

  • Team arrives at ground 2 hours early
  • Inspect pitch and ground conditions
  • Warm-up routine (30 minutes)
  • Captain and coach finalize playing XI
  • Brief team on opposition and game plan
  • Mental preparation and team huddle

Batting Strategy

Phase Overs Strategy Target Run Rate
Powerplay 1-10 Build foundation, rotate strike, attack loose balls 4.5-5.0 per over
Middle Overs 11-30 Partnerships, rotate strike, target weak bowlers 4.0-4.5 per over
Death Overs 31-40 Accelerate, target boundaries, maximize score 6.0+ per over

Bowling Strategy

Phase Overs Bowlers Strategy
New Ball 1-10 Saravana S, Sunit Sar Attack stumps, take early wickets, tight lines
Middle Overs 11-30 Harish Shetty, Mohammed Zubair, Raghav Pyaraka Build pressure, vary pace, restrict runs
Death Overs 31-40 Best bowlers (Saravana S, Sunit Sar) Yorkers, slower balls, defensive fields

Fielding Positions & Tactics

  • Attacking Fields (1-15 overs): 3 slips, gully, point, cover, mid-off, mid-on, fine leg
  • Defensive Fields (16-35 overs): Spread field, save boundaries, force singles
  • Death Overs (36-40): Boundary riders, protect straight boundaries
  • Key Positions: Best fielders at point, cover, and mid-wicket
  • Communication: Constant talk between fielders and bowlers

Post-Match Review

  • Immediate team huddle after match
  • Discuss what worked and what didn't
  • Individual player feedback sessions
  • Video analysis of key moments
  • Update opposition intelligence database
  • Plan adjustments for next match
