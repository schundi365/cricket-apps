🏋️ Net Training Techniques for Performance Improvement

🏏 Batting Training Drills

Technical Foundation

  • Throw-downs: 50 balls focusing on straight drive technique
  • Tee-ball practice: Static ball hitting for timing
  • Wall practice: 15 mins daily for hand-eye coordination
  • Mirror work: Shadow batting for muscle memory

Situational Batting

  • Target practice: Hit to specific areas (covers, mid-wicket)
  • Pressure scenarios: 10 runs needed off 6 balls
  • Bad ball punishment: Wide/short ball practice
  • Running between wickets: Quick singles drill

Match Simulation

  • Bowling machine: Varying pace and length
  • Live bowling: 6-over spells with field restrictions
  • Partnership drills: Two batsmen, target scores
  • Fatigue batting: Batting after physical exercise

🎳 Bowling Training Drills

Line & Length

  • Target bowling: Cones on good length spot
  • Stump practice: 6 balls at stumps from 22 yards
  • Corridor drill: Bowl in 4th-5th stump channel
  • Rhythm bowling: 30 balls continuous, same spot

Variations & Control

  • Slower balls: Practice 3 different variations
  • Yorker practice: 20 yorkers at base of stumps
  • Spin bowling: Turn and bounce on different surfaces
  • Field setting: Bowl to specific field placements

Death Bowling

  • Wide yorkers: Outside off-stump practice
  • Pressure bowling: Batsman trying to hit boundaries
  • Plan execution: Bowl to pre-decided plans
  • Recovery bowling: After being hit for boundaries

🥅 Fielding Training Drills
